# Design: Takt for students

This app speaks bestanden's design language (`~/code/bestandenfmsneufeld/DESIGN.md`, itself yaft's), as cbra-platform's account site ports it: opaque paper leaves with 2px ink outlines and short, hard ink shadows; Bricolage Grotesque names things, Manrope says and counts them; a full-strength hue is the page. Every token is a CSS variable in `src/app.css`. This record holds only what the app adds or changes.

## Courses, not years

bestanden's year hues become one hue per course: Marigold, Lagoon Teal, Lilac, Flamingo (`--hue-0` to `--hue-3`), given to the student's courses in order of their names and repeating after four. `data-hue` on `<html>` sets `--hue` while a lesson is shown, and the hue is the page. A course's Verlauf and Demnächst, and an assessment's details page, take their course's hue too. Without any of them on show (sign-in, the overview, settings, the empty state) the page is Fog Ground. A course is always named next to its hue.

## Dark theme

Follows the system setting. Ink and paper swap as in bestanden; the page is the course hue mixed 24% into the dark ground, and the overview's hue strips stay full strength.

## Screens

- **Sign-in:** a narrow column (460px): the wordmark, "Anmelden" as the display title, a lede, one leaf with email, password and the primary button, then "Passwort vergessen" and "Konto erstellen" as links under it. A refusal is an error box inside the leaf.
- **Lesson:** the app bar (wordmark; "Übersicht", in the desktop app a quiet "Einstellungen", and a quiet "Abmelden"; on the overview and the empty state, the same without "Übersicht"), then the head on the hue: course, day and time separated by ink dots, and the topic as the display title ("Noch kein Thema" without one). Under it, from 10 minutes before the start until the end (by the device's clock), the primary "Bereit" button; a refusal is an error box under it ("Check-in ist erst ab 08:05 möglich."). Once checked in, it becomes a paper chip with a 2px ink outline and a short ink shadow, "Eingecheckt 08:17 · pünktlich" or "· zu spät" (only the time when a mark of the teacher's decides), which stays on the lesson after it ends. While the exit ticket is open, the "Exit-Ticket" leaf comes next (see below). Then one leaf each for "Ziele", "Wo wir stehen", "Hausaufgaben" and "Material", each only when it has content, and "Letzte Lektion": its day and time in caption type, its topic, then its homework and materials as groups titled over a 2px ink rule.
- **Exit ticket:** a leaf titled "Exit-Ticket". On top a note box says who sees what: "«Was war unklar?» geht mit deinem Namen an Anna Muster. «Was haben wir gemacht?» siehst nur du. Wenn du anwesend bist, zählt das Abgeben für die mündliche Note." ("deine Lehrperson" without a name). Then two textareas in the input style, each under a field label that says who reads it ("· nur für dich", "· an Anna Muster, mit deinem Namen"); under the second an "Alles klar" checkbox that greys the textarea out. Then, when the lesson has goals, one row per goal: its text in label weight, and five square 44px chips "–", 1, 2, 3, 4 with 2px ink outlines and short shadows, the chosen one filled with ink ("–", no rating, by default). Last the primary "Abgeben" with "Offen bis 09:15" in muted type beside it, or, once handed in, "Abgegeben 08:52 · du kannst bis 09:15 ändern" in bold; a refusal is an error box above the button. Once closed, the leaf sits after the lesson's content: a note box "Das Exit-Ticket ist seit 09:15 geschlossen. Abgegeben 08:52." (or "Du hast keines abgegeben.") and the answers handed in, each under a small label ("Was haben wir gemacht? · nur für dich", "Was war unklar?", each rated goal with "3 von 4").
- **Anonymous review:** when the lesson has one, the exit-ticket leaf ends with a part of its own, apart from the named form: "Anonym" as a group titled over a 2px ink rule, a note box "Deine Lehrperson sieht nicht, wer das geschrieben hat.", then "Wie war die Lektion für dich? · 1 = gar nicht gut, 5 = sehr gut" in label weight with five square chips 1–5 in the goal ratings' style (none chosen at first), the lesson's two prompts as textareas under field labels ending "· freiwillig", and its own primary "Anonym abgeben" with "Nur einmal, ohne Ändern · offen bis 09:15" in muted type beside it; a refusal is an error box above the button. Once answered, only "Abgegeben" in bold (with the error box "Du hast diese Bewertung schon abgegeben." when a second answer was refused); never the answer. Once closed, unanswered: "Die Bewertung ist seit 09:15 geschlossen." in muted type. The exit ticket's form styles (text, the numbered chips, the button row) live in `src/app.css`, shared by both parts.
- **Overview:** "Übersicht" (with a lesson on) or "Gerade keine Lektion" as the title, then cards: the current lesson ("Jetzt"), the next lesson of each course and the last lesson. A card is a paper leaf with a 14px strip of its course's hue on the left, a caption with the kind and the course, the topic in Bricolage and the day and time. The current and the last lesson open on click; a next lesson's card doesn't open, so of its content only the topic shows. Under the cards, "Prüfungen und Abgaben" as a leaf title, when any lie ahead: up to five cards like the lessons', captioned with the kind and the course, the title in Bricolage and the day, for an Abgabe with "· noch 3 Tage"; each opens its details page. Then "Deine Kurse": one card per course (by name), the course name in Bricolage and two plain buttons, "Demnächst" and "Verlauf".
- **Demnächst:** on the course's hue: the course name as the meta line, "Demnächst" as the display title, then a muted line, "Bis Ende Semester, Freitag, 29. Januar." or "Alle kommenden Lektionen." without a school year. Then one leaf per week, "Woche vom 9. November" as its title; in it each day in caption type, its lessons as rows (the times in ink-2, the topic in bold, never more) with the day's badges, and each Prüfung or Abgabe as a button on the hue wash with a 2px ink outline and short shadow: its badge, the title in bold and, for an Abgabe, the countdown in muted type. It opens the details page. Without anything ahead, a lede says so ("Bis Ende Semester ist nichts mehr geplant.", or "Noch nichts geplant.").
- **Badges:** "Prüfung" as a small ink-filled pill, "Abgabe" as a paper pill with a 2px ink outline, both in 12px extra-bold. On a lesson in Demnächst, in Verlauf under its topic and in the lesson view under the title; in Verlauf and the lesson view they are buttons (44px tall) that open the details page.
- **Assessment details:** on the course's hue: course, kind and day as the meta line, the title as the display title, for an Abgabe "Abgabe bis Freitag, 13. November, 18:00 · **noch 3 Tage**" (the line in semibold, the countdown in bold) ("noch 5 Std. 20 Min." under a day, "abgelaufen" after it), then a plain button "Demnächst in 3a · Informatik". Then a leaf each, when filled in: "Stoff" (the covered pages as material links), "Ziele" (a list), "Zum Üben" (the practice link as "Übungsaufgaben") and "Hinweise" (the free text, line breaks kept, its web addresses linked). With none of them, a note box "Details folgen."
- **Verlauf:** on the course's hue: the course name as the meta line, "Verlauf" as the display title, then "Als Markdown speichern" as a plain button with a muted line beside it ("Deine Notizen und Exit-Tickets, auch nach dem Archivieren des Kurses."); a failed save is an error box under it. Then one leaf per past lesson, newest first: day and time in caption type, the topic in Bricolage (`--t-xl`), then a row of paper chips with 2px ink outlines and short shadows: the student's status ("pünktlich", "zu spät", "entschuldigt", "anwesend", "verpasst") and the ticket's ("Abgegeben 08:52", "Exit-Ticket entschuldigt", "Exit-Ticket offen bis 09:15"). A lesson from before the course's check-in start has neither status chip nor ticket chip (only "Abgegeben" if the student handed one in), and no highlight. A missed lesson's leaf sits on the danger tint with its "verpasst" chip filled with ink, so it stands out to catch up on; "Exit-Ticket fehlt" is a chip in danger red (outline, shadow and text). Then homework, materials and "Dein Exit-Ticket" as groups titled over a 2px ink rule, the answers each under a small label as on the closed exit ticket. Without past lessons, a lede says so.
- **Settings:** in the desktop app only, from "Einstellungen" in the app bar, where "Zurück" replaces "Übersicht" and "Einstellungen" and leads home, on Fog Ground: "Einstellungen" as the display title, then a leaf "Benachrichtigungen" with four switches, each a checkbox in the "Alles klar" style (all on by default) with a muted line under it on when it fires: «Exit-Ticket ist offen», «Prüfung/Abgabe in 7 Tagen», «Prüfung/Abgabe morgen» and «Abgabe heute». A last muted line says Takt notifies only when the app runs that day.
- **Notification:** in the desktop app only, an OS notification "Exit-Ticket ist offen" ("Exit-Ticket ist offen – mit Bewertung" when the lesson has an anonymous review) with the course and "offen bis 09:15", once per lesson when the exit ticket opens, unless it's handed in or switched off. Reminders of Prüfungen and Abgaben the same way, titled "Prüfung in 7 Tagen", "Abgabe morgen" or "Abgabe heute", with the course, the title and an Abgabe's "bis 18:00" ("bis Freitag, 20. November, 18:00" when the deadline is on another day).
- **Empty:** "Noch keine Lektionen" as the title and a lede saying lessons appear once the teacher plans them.
- **Offline:** a note above the content, "Keine Verbindung. Stand 08:15.", while the last lessons read stay on screen.
- **Update:** in the release desktop app only, a leaf fixed to the bottom right (460px at most): "Neue Version von Takt", the version and that Takt restarts, then a quiet "Später" and the primary "Aktualisieren" ("Wird installiert …" while it runs). A failure is an error box inside the leaf.

## Install page

`installieren.html` (`/installieren` on the web), on Fog Ground with the app's tokens (`src/install.css`): the wordmark, "Takt installieren" as the display title and a lede; two download leaves side by side (macOS, Windows), each a caption, the platform name in Bricolage and a primary "Herunterladen"; a note on the one-time approval; then one leaf each for the macOS and Windows steps (numbered, the button names in bold and «»), "Updates" and "Im Browser".

## Material links

Each material is a link in label weight with a "↗" after it, at least 44px tall. A path the app can't link (no site URL for the course) is shown as plain text.
